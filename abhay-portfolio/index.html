Case study 1AI Design and Automation

Company
Z21 TechLabs
Client
Palak Designers
Scope
AI imagery, video, web design, automation

Turning raw, unedited phone photos into an editorial, high‑fashion brand

Palak Designers is a wholesale kurti and ethnic wear label. I redesigned its social media with AI-generated editorial imagery and short-form video, designed a minimalist website, and automated how new posts are produced.

The website

A minimalist site for the brand. The homepage opens on a festive banner, then leads into everyday and designer collections, exclusive boutique pieces and matching mother-and-daughter sets, with a clear way to ask for a quote at every step.

Scroll inside the frame to see the whole homepage.

Social posts

  • The brief

    The brand's social feed relied on unedited product photos, and it had no website. Palak Designers wanted a modern, consistent look on Instagram and a simple site to show its collections.

  • What I did

    • Editorial imagery. Turned raw photos from the store and warehouse into hyper-realistic AI model shoots, with every garment kept exactly as it is.
    • Social posts. Redesigned the post layouts so each product's details and colour options read clearly and consistently.
    • Video. Generated AI video clips and edited them into Instagram-ready reels.
    • Website. Designed a minimalist website for the brand's collections.
    • Automation. Automated post generation, so each new product drops into the fixed layout without being designed by hand.

  • Challenges

    • Keeping the garment untouched. The source photos were shot on a phone, in poor light and at low quality. Each prompt had to lift the image to editorial quality without changing the kurti's cut, colour, print or embroidery.
    • Video without hallucinations. Instead of one long generation, I produced several 5-second clips. Shorter clips used fewer tokens and gave the AI less room for mistakes, and I stitched them into one seamless reel.

From raw photo to post and reel

Every product went through the same three steps: raw phone photos from the store, an AI editorial image for the post, then an AI reel built from 5-second clips.

Case study 2UI UX Design

Company
EY (Ernst & Young)
Client
EY internal teams
Scope
Dashboards, internal tools, sites and forms

Designing working tools for EY's internal teams at delivery pace

A run of internal products for EY teams: a demand-and-capacity dashboard, a data-dictionary accelerator, an experiments and segments landing page, and the everyday sites and forms that keep delivery moving.

Demand-and-capacity dashboard

Data-dictionary accelerator

Experiments and segments landing page

  • The brief

    EY's internal teams needed working tools they could use straight away, designed and handed over at the pace of live delivery. Alongside three larger products there was a steady stream of internal sites and forms.

  • What I did

    • Demand-and-capacity dashboard. Designed a dashboard that sets incoming demand against available team capacity, so leads can spot gaps early and plan resourcing at a glance.
    • Data-dictionary accelerator. Designed a tool that turns scattered data definitions into one searchable reference, so teams can find, understand and reuse data faster.
    • Experiments and segments landing page. Designed a single landing page for experiments and audience segments, so teams can see what's running and who it's aimed at.
    • Everyday sites and forms. Designed internal sites and forms for routine requests and information, kept consistent so people don't have to relearn each one.
    • Delivery pace. Worked in short cycles alongside the delivery teams, handing over build-ready screens that follow EY's design standards.

  • Challenges

    • Designing inside strict brand standards. Every screen had to follow EY's design system and brand rules, with little room for new patterns or visual styles. The work was about finding clarity inside those limits, through hierarchy, layout and content rather than decoration.

Project
Book design
Subject
Dog nutrition

The Canine Kitchen Encyclopedia

A kitchen encyclopedia for anyone who has a dog or loves them: the nutritious foods to feed, and the toxic ones to keep well out of reach.

  • The idea

    A canine kitchen encyclopedia for anyone who has a pet or loves them. It brings together the nutritious foods worth feeding and the toxic ones to keep a dog away from, so owners can make safe choices with confidence.

  • Designed for quick answers

    The layout is built to get readers to the information as quickly and easily as possible. Each food sits on its own clearly marked card, so the answer is visible at a glance instead of buried in paragraphs.

  • Tips and warning signs

    Each section gives readers tips and tricks for spotting the signs to watch out for, for safe and toxic foods alike. Toxic entries walk through the harmful part, why it's dangerous, the symptoms to look for, a nutritionist's note and the precaution to take.
